### About

I'm the founder of **Gravity Concepts** — a founder-led creative and tech studio. We close the gap between how good a business's work is and how it looks online, then engineer the systems that turn visitors into enquiries.

I work full-stack — React, Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Django — so clients get one accountable partner across brand, print, web, and hosting. Not five disconnected vendors.

- 🛠️ **Currently building** — Mercora, a SaaS storefront platform for African merchants
- 📐 **Approach** — discovery before design, structure before execution, one clear CTA
- 🌍 **Based in** — Anambra State, Nigeria. Working with clients in Nigeria and internationally.
- ✅ **250+ projects delivered** since 2022 — single-page sites to production SaaS platforms
